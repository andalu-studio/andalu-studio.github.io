Andalu

Calm, slow videos for ages 3–6. Each episode teaches one small thing — counting, colors, feelings — and begins and ends with one slow breath.

Videos are made with AI tools (script help, synthetic narration, code-drawn animation) and every one is reviewed by a person before it is published.
